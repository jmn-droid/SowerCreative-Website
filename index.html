Common Questions

How long until I see results? +

Most clients see their first leads within the first week of launch. Profitable campaigns typically take 30–60 days — the first month is about learning and optimization, not scaling. We'll be honest with you about the timeline from day one.

What ad budget do I need? +

We recommend a minimum ad spend of $1,000–$1,500/month to give the algorithm enough data to optimize effectively. Less than that and you're fighting the learning phase. We'll talk through your specific situation on the strategy call.

Do I need to be on camera for the videos? +

For most small businesses, yes — and it's actually a huge advantage. People buy from people. Face-to-camera content outperforms faceless content on Meta almost every time. We make it easy: we write the scripts, you read them. No experience needed.

What if I've run ads before and they didn't work? +

That's actually most of our clients. Failed ad campaigns are usually a strategy or creative problem, not a platform problem. We'll look at what you ran before, diagnose what went wrong, and build from there. Meta works — when the fundamentals are right.

How is this different from other marketing agencies? +

We're small by design. We don't onboard dozens of clients and hand them to a junior account manager. You work directly with Joeden and Logan. We're focused exclusively on Meta — not trying to be everything to everyone. And we're honest when something isn't working, which isn't as common as it should be.
