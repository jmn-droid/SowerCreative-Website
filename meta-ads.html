How It Works

From call to
campaign in two weeks.

01

Strategy Call

We start with a 30-minute call to understand your business, your ideal customer, and what a great lead looks like for you. If we're a good fit, we move forward. If not, we'll say so.

02

Research & Build

We spend the first two weeks on research -- audience analysis, competitor ads, offer positioning. Then we build your campaign from the ground up before a single dollar goes to Meta.

03

Launch & Learn

We launch and watch everything closely. The first 30 days are about data -- understanding what's resonating, where leads are dropping off, and what the numbers are telling us.

04

Scale What Works

Once we know what's converting, we scale it. Budget goes toward what the data supports. Every month we refine the targeting, test new creative, and push toward a lower cost per lead.
