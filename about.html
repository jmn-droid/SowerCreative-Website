Sower Creative is a two-person marketing agency focused on one thing: getting small businesses real results on Meta. We believe every dollar you invest should produce fruit.

Our Story

We started small.
On purpose.

Sower Creative started with a simple observation: most small businesses either can't afford a big agency, or they've been burned by one that overpromised and underdelivered. We wanted to build something different.

We're not trying to manage a hundred clients. We take on a small number of businesses we genuinely believe in, and we pour everything into getting them results. That means tighter relationships, more accountability, and more care about whether your campaign is actually working.

The name comes from the parable of the sower — the idea that good seed, planted in the right soil, produces an extraordinary harvest. That's the work we do. We help you find your audience, craft your message, and plant it where it grows.
